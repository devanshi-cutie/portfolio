The creator

A Jaipur-based storyteller turning ordinary moments — an outfit, a skincare step, a plate of food — into content that feels like a message from a friend.

Based between old-city lanes and new-market cafés, she creates fashion, beauty and lifestyle content built around one idea: it should feel real before it looks polished. Every reel starts as a genuine reaction, then gets the pacing, light and sound that make brands want to repost it. What sets the work apart is the mix — a Pink City point of view, shot with a big-city production sense.
